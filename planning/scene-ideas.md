# Gizmo's Wall — Scene Idea Board

**Started:** November 15, 2026  
**Live idea board:** https://www.andydlin.com/muse-daily-outfits/ideas.html  
**Related planning:** [MapleStory Hero × Boss Pairings](maplestory-pairings.md) · [Live pairing tracker](https://www.andydlin.com/muse-daily-outfits/pairings.html)

An ongoing parking lot for future dioramas. Entries are **ideas, not approved production commitments**. Keep them here until references are collected, composition is proposed, and the user approves an individual image.

## 01 · Boss spotlights — cubby and island
**Status:** Idea · Not started

Create dioramas featuring a **single recognizable MapleStory boss**, with no hero required. Explore the same boss in **both cubby and floating-island** presentation formats. Focus on boss personality, scale, signature attacks, arena architecture, and a distinctive environment. Boss examples and format choices are intentionally open.

## 02 · Mobs and nostalgic MapleStory locations
**Status:** Idea · Not started

Create small world-building dioramas centered on mobs and their habitats instead of hero-vs-boss combat.

- **Slime:** A playful, recognizable slime-centric scene.
- **Slime Tree:** A nostalgic tree setting with slimes in their environment.
- **Pigs at the beach farm:** A beach/farm-inspired scene with pigs and environmental storytelling.

Possible presentations: both cubbies and floating islands, selected during individual scene planning.

## 03 · Multi-hero boss encounters
**Status:** Idea · Not started

Depict **multiple heroes together in one scene**, such as two or more MapleStory heroes teaming up against a boss. Explore complementary classes, coordinated abilities, readable character silhouettes, balanced action, and a shared combat arena. Exact hero teams, boss choices, and scene format will be selected later.

## Workflow notes

All scenes inherit the current Jolly and MapleStory specifications and the applicable cubby or island standards. Before any image generation or edit, consult current GitHub prompts, collect needed references, propose the scene, and await explicit approval. The idea board does not override existing hero/boss pairing records or approved artwork.
