## Scene-specific reference packets and proposed direction (not yet approved)

### Nova 01 — Kaiser vs Magnus

**Hero:** Kaiser armor/cape/horns, two-handed sword construction and correct two-hand grip; check ordinary armored form versus transformed form and pick one before composing.  
**Skill candidates:** Gigas Wave (verify current effect/animation), sword impact and Dragon Link/Final Form visual cues; select one based on actual references.  
**Boss:** Magnus sword, wing arrangement, purple energy, aerial stance, recognizable in-game attack.  
**Map:** Heliseum throne/ruins and characteristic masonry.  
**Composition candidate:** Kaiser foreground grounded sword swing; Magnus mid-background, airborne and countering; reserve unobstructed floor in front of hero; one dominant opposing energy effect.  
**Critical check:** Do not replace Kaiser's real sword with a magic streak; large wing silhouette must not intersect LED or cubby frame.

### Nova 02 — Angelic Buster vs Lucid

**Hero:** transformed pink idol costume, Soul Shooter geometry and how it is held; decide on official face/hair and costume version.  
**Skill candidates:** Trinity or Sparkle Burst; confirm exact action/effect palette from current in-game captures.  
**Boss:** Lucid floating silhouette, butterfly effects, selected dream-phase details, physical outfit/hair, recognizable attack animation.  
**Map:** Lachelein / Lucid dream realm architecture (dream forest, clocktower or ornamental ruins, choose accurate visual reference).  
**Composition candidate:** Angelic Buster in clear airborne attack pose, Soul Shooter pointed toward Lucid in mid-background; strongly distinguish pink hero from purple/lavender boss.  
**Critical check:** Avoid undifferentiated pastel effects and oversized butterfly clutter; hero's weapon grip stays readable.

### Nova 03 — Cadena vs Magnus

**Hero:** Cadena's costume, physical chain and handle/grip, anchor/weapon arrangement, movement poses; distinguish chain from additional summoned weapons.  
**Skill candidates:** Chain Arts: Beatdown or a verified chain-attack frame with readable physical tension and direction.  
**Boss:** Same canonical Magnus design as Nova 01 unless user approves alternate form; one contrasting attack or wing pose.  
**Map:** Ruined Heliseum royal courtyard; distinguish from Kaiser fortress in set dressing, camera and geometry.  
**Composition candidate:** Cadena foreground/midair at diagonal, chain reaching toward a secondary-background hovering Magnus; retain plausible chain continuity and floor depth.  
**Critical check:** No impossible hand-to-chain connection; no chain disappearing behind props; clearly different palette, boss pose and camera from Kaiser pairing.

### Nova 04 — Kain vs Kaling

**Hero:** Kain hood, crossbow geometry, exact aiming grip, Malice/Thanatos effects and current action frames.  
**Skill candidates:** Thanatos Descent or a verified possession/execute crossbow skill; select after reference review.  
**Boss:** Kaling's exact form and one selected Peril/guardian with official reference; decide whether to feature Kaling alone or one subordinate beast without making scene unreadable.  
**Map:** Shangri-La and Kaling arena architecture, mountains, terraces, clouds and effects.  
**Composition candidate:** Kain foreground leaning backward while bracing/aiming toward a clearly readable Kaling presence; one dominant boss-associated attack in mid-background.  
**Critical check:** Crossbow must remain a real weapon with correct hand placement; do not merge multiple boss forms into an invented monster. Keep boss and beast readable within cubby depth.
