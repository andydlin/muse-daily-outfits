# Daily Outfits — Create & Redo (consolidated V2)

**Read with:** ../shared/jolly-character.md, ../shared/cubby-lighting.md, ../shared/plush-materials.md

## Before generating
1. Ask/offer a concrete scene plan (pose, main action, mini environment, lighting/effects) and **wait for user approval** even if an outfit reference is supplied.
2. User supplies outfit/theme references and ideally current approved Jolly and cubby visual masters.
3. Always use **“inspired”** in the character/theme descriptor.

## Create — template

Create **ONE individual square Gizmo's Wall image**: **[CHARACTER] inspired — [SCENE]**.

**Approved scene plan:** [INSERT APPROVED POSE, GESTURE, ACTION, GROUND, SCENERY, CAMERA AND EFFECT NOTES].

Produce a premium photorealistic 3D handmade plush miniature diorama, with a recognizable compact Muse Jolly mascot. Apply the FULL attached Jolly identity standard: broad smooth finely velvety cream inset face separate from fluffier cream framing fur, small spaced black bead eyes, soft blush, tiny simple mouth, no anatomical ears, compact body. Outfit details come from the supplied costume references; never adopt their 2D art/rendering style. Costume hair is dimensional textile tufts, never flat. Creature-hood eyes/features are embroidered **on the hood**, leaving Jolly's face visible.

Apply the shared tactile miniature material standard across costume, environment and props. Use the same complete, rigid, beveled sharp-cornered square peach/ivory cubby, exterior wall, soft shadow, recessed interior depth, and **small thin flush-centered warm-white ceiling LED** found in the approved daily gallery reference. Keep all four outer frame edges visible, consistent square margins, lighting softness and natural depth. Keep magical effects readable, not overblown.

**Do not include** Meta branding, text, titles, numbering, plaques, labels, checklist, UI controls or any progress overlay in the image. Return the single image, not a collage or side-by-side.

## Redo — template

**EDIT THE PROVIDED EXISTING IMAGE, not a new scene.** Subject: [CHARACTER] inspired — [SCENE].

**Requested changes only:** [EXACT EDITS].

Maintain the approved Jolly identity, clothing, action, gesture, cubby and visual material style. Keep all unrelated elements **as unchanged as possible**, including face if not requested, outfit, hair, prop locations, camera, framing, lighting, background and effects. Recolor only via the dedicated recolor workflow. Show one revised image; no comparison unless requested.

## After output

Keep any scene checklist **in chat, not inside the image**. If presenting Create/Redo checklist controls, they must copy editable prompts **without automatically sending them** or launching image generation.

## Daily Outfit face construction — first-pass lock

**Use `MASTER_Jolly_Face_Jett.png` primary and `MASTER_Jolly_Face_Secondy_Goku.png` secondary.** Before generation, describe the face as a **distinct broad smooth velvety cream inner oval** surrounded by a **clearly visible slightly fluffier cream head-fur ring**. The two surfaces must differ in fiber length, contour and material finish; no uniformly furry face, coarse bouclé, flat painted border or shiny vinyl. Sculpted costume hair stays separate from both layers.

**Expression follows the approved scene:** serious, angry, shouting, sleeping and happy expressions are all valid. Preserve approved brows, gaze and mouth; never automatically turn a fierce Jolly into a smiling one. Small Jolly-style eyes and rounded underlying proportions remain recognizable. Daily Outfit faces must not inherit MapleStory-specific eye/expression styling.

**Face-only repair:** localize edits to the smooth inset, cream fur-ring separation and only the expression features explicitly requested. Keep all other approved scene elements as unchanged as possible. The approved Yuji Itadori — Black Flash Option 1 illustrates successful separation with a serious expression but is **not** a new master or a change in reference priority.

## First-pass quality checks — mandatory

**Jolly face validation:** Compare against Jett (primary) and Goku (secondary). Confirm (1) smooth, softly contoured cream inner facial inset, (2) distinct fluffier cream outer head ring, (3) separate dimensional costume hair, (4) original rounded Jolly proportions and restrained eyes, and (5) exact scene-approved emotion, including fierce angled brows or shouting mouth where called for. Reject a uniformly furry face or a correction that makes a serious character cheerful. Wild Hunter is a texture reference, not a substitute Daily Outfit face reference.

**Cubby validation:** After generation, inspect the image against `MASTER_Cubby_Bulbasaur.png` (primary) and `MASTER_Cubby_Secondary_Link.png` (secondary) using the checklist in `../shared/cubby-lighting.md`. Confirm centered full-frame geometry, generous and balanced **exterior wall visible on all four sides**, neutral ivory-cream bevels, a softly feathered exterior shadow, the small flush centered LED, and warm—but not excessively orange—lighting. A strong sunset, sandstorm, fire, or dramatic glow may affect the interior, but must not erase exterior wall margins or tint the whole frame pink/orange.

**Creature hood validation:** For a creature-themed hood, verify the theme's recognizable embroidered eyes, pupils, highlights and markings are visibly **on the hood**, with Jolly's original cream face unobstructed. Preserve approved silhouette details such as spikes, horns, ears, armor or tail according to the supplied reference. Do not invent the same markings for unrelated creatures.

If only the cubby fails validation, correct **only** cubby geometry, frame/wall color, LED and architectural lighting/shadow; do not regenerate or reinterpret approved interior scene artwork, face, costume, pose or effects. Keep one output per iteration.
