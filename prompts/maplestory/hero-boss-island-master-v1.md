# Hero × Boss Island — Master Scale & Camera Spec v1

> **Approved island material override (2026-11-23):** Standalone islands use premium handcrafted miniature collectible figures, not cubby plush characters. Use `MASTER_Island_Composition_V1.jpeg` for the visual direction; a dedicated island character texture master is pending selection. Hair: dimensional sculpted locks with subtle directional grooves, not yarn or dense fibers. Face: smooth matte anime face, minimal grain, class-specific eyes. Clothing: restrained fine fabric, seams and stitching; no coarse repetitive weave. Armor and weapons: satin-matte miniature metal; leather: subtle grain; fur: short controlled fibers where appropriate. Avoid glossy plastic and excessive fuzz. Wild Hunter and Mechanic are supplemental material references only, never island face, scale or composition authorities. This override supersedes conflicting plush guidance for islands only; cubby rules remain unchanged.


**Status:** User-approved design direction, 2026-11-20. Visual baseline sheets and master render are not yet generated or approved. **Applies to NEW Hero × Boss encounter islands**; does not retroactively change approved existing island art.

## Identity
Create a cohesive floating miniature object with a clean, readable MapleStory combat encounter. Separate future environment-led **MapleStory Worlds** islands into a distinct collection; those may use more scenery and atmospheric perspective. Island hero faces follow [floating-islands.md](floating-islands.md), **not** cubby Jolly face rules. Use class-specific references and the Wild Hunter material benchmark. No text/UI in final artwork.

## Proposed numeric target — approved as a design goal, verify against Blender blueprint before hard camera lock
- 1254 × 1254 square; final flat backdrop #F3E6DD.
- Island footprint: 74–78% image width, centered, with approximately 11–13% margins on each side.
- Combat plane/front region centered about 62–66% of image height.
- Visible top-floor region target: about 14–18% of image height (subject to **measurement definition** and visual camera check).
- Underside target depth: 16–20% of image height, broad shoulders curving into a tapered central bottom.
- Scenery breathing space above tallest objects: ideally 10–14%.
- Camera: low, shallow, nearly side-on, with modest downward visibility of a continuous, level oval deck; combat contacts share one physical plane. Avoid a top-down appearance.
- Standard standing hero silhouette: approximately 21–25% of full image height, allowing pose-specific differences. Hero face, costume and weapon should read at gallery size.
- Use rock/brick/door/tree/pillar proportions anchored to the hero for **nearby** scenery. Smaller distant background elements are allowed when perspective and softer contrast clearly indicate distance.

**Important existing-guidance conflict:** [floating-islands.md](floating-islands.md) documents the existing Blender kit baseline around 83% width, 7° down, y~814 front rim and 52–53 px visible deck depth (roughly 4% of canvas). These are **not numerically equivalent** to the new 74–78% and 14–18% targets. Do not claim the Blender kit matches v1 automatically or silently distort existing masters. For a NEW encounter, produce and compare an actual Blender camera blockout at matched square resolution; reconcile the visible-floor definition, screen position, island size, and angle with the user's approved reference imagery before declaring a new locked camera. For an ALREADY-approved island, its locked approved Blender camera and existing layers take precedence until a separate revision is approved.

## Spatial hierarchy
- Foreground: clear action lane with feet/hover grounding, readable hero and boss, and modest paving in front of characters.
- Midground: moderate map-specific scenery along the far curved rim; rocks, ruins and vegetation can wrap around front corners without blocking silhouettes.
- Background: optional, low-contrast, softly receding atmospheric scenery; borrow the old-school side-view depth of reference #2 sparingly, never its crowding.
- Source comparison priorities: image #6's character/environment balance and wraparound scenery; #7's battle clarity; #5's effect integration; #2's restrained distant depth. Avoid #4's emptiness and the too-small brick scale in #5/#7. Preserve the distinctive colors and textures of actual maps rather than drifting to an unrelated palette.

## Relative scale categories, anchored to the same hero
- Compact/humanoid boss: approximately 95–130% of hero height.
- Large creature boss: approximately 125–180% of hero height.
- Colossal boss: may exceed 180%; show a selective emerged/coiled/partial body rather than shrinking hero to fit.
- Mounted/vehicle hero: judge the total combined silhouette; ensure rider remains identifiable. Smaller summons are secondary.
These are staging starting points, not mandatory transformations of source character designs.

## Visual scale reference kit (planned; requires per-image approval before generation)
1. Character & boss scale sheet: same hero baseline, compact/large/colossal boss and mounted/vehicle cases, same ground line and camera; clearly distinguish reference imagery from final artwork.
2. Environment scale sheet: same hero with near-field bricks, boulders, pillars, arch, door, tree and props; show near vs distant scale cues; detect dollhouse brick drift.
3. Island camera sheet: actual approved Blender geometry with empty deck and stand-ins, showing projected top-floor strip, front rim, underside, clearance, scenery wrap and action lane. This is **not** a freehand camera substitute.

Plan and obtain explicit user approval before rendering any reference sheet. Reference sheets may include instructional annotations if requested, but **final gallery artwork never contains text, UI, plaques or labels**. After the visuals are approved, use them as versioned masters rather than replacing earlier approved references silently.
