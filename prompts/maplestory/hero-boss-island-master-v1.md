## Floating presentation — no exterior shadow
The island floats freely in an uninterrupted background. No ground plane, exterior cast shadow, grounding shadow, drop shadow or reflection beneath the island. Preserve natural internal contact shadows between characters, scenery and terrain, and natural shading on the island underside.

## Spatial hierarchy
Use the approved composition master for balance, open combat lane and environmental depth, not as a universal map design. Keep nearby architecture and props believable relative to heroes; use overlap, smaller distant forms and subtle atmospheric depth. Preserve map identity. No floating fragments or pasted-on scenery.
