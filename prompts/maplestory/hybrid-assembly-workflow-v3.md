## Preferred workflow
For new hero generation, follow the [Hero Generation Prompt](hero-generation-prompt.md) and [Hero Style Guide](hero-style-guide-v0-1.md). Original character artwork controls identity only, not anatomy or pose by default. Approve independent miniature anatomy references and character-specific proportion targets before generation; validate anatomy before foundation approval or component refinement. Preserve already approved character anatomy during insertion. Record prompt revision and resolved inputs; disclose reusable prompt changes and obtain approval, commit and verify before adoption. No universal ratio is introduced.

1. Gather encounter references, propose composition and obtain approval. Approve composition-aware blueprint before foundation generation: posed silhouettes, bounding boxes, footing/hover anchors, equipment/wing/tail clearance, combat lane and visible scenery. Classify integrated, mounted, mechanical and independent assets; environment-only scenes need no placeholders.
2. Generate cohesive foundation with connected terrain and integrated bosses where appropriate; no planning placeholders. Proportionally normalize and approve foundation before independent insertion.
3. Refine measured placement blueprint on approved foundation. Extract clean padded crops and matching silhouette guides programmatically. Record exact source dimensions, origin, padding, anchors and intended scale. Choose context and padding per encounter; avoid clipping equipment or shadows. Plan overlapping crops explicitly.
4. Generate one localized edit per approved step. Clean crop is sole editing target; sprite controls identity; silhouette controls placement; material references control materials only; separately approved anatomy references control anatomy. Use concise prompts, preserve framing, scenery, perspective and lighting; no annotations or effects during validation. Introduce references incrementally when troubleshooting. Fresh external chat and iOS Photos upload are optional workarounds, not requirements; file-access error was observed but causation unconfirmed.
5. Approve each character independently, comparing Fighter head/body proportions, identity, anatomy, equipment/grip, materials, pose, footing/hover and lighting. Preserve every approved source. Do not regenerate other characters to refine one.
6. Normalize generated crops to recorded dimensions without distortion; check landmark registration, silhouette bounds, anchors and relative scale. Create and approve measured structural composite as authority for final arrangement; generated crops do not override it. Reject unacceptable drift or return to approved assets.
7. Reinsert crop interiors programmatically, preserving naturally integrated character, contact shadows and immediate surroundings. Prefer perimeter feathering over character-edge masking when appropriate. Compare direct insertion and adjustable feather widths; 40 original-canvas px is a starting point only. Protect character and shadow regions; inspect overlap, seams, ghosting and landmark mismatch. Preserve pixels outside affected regions where feasible and verify before claiming identity. Never claim regenerated scenery is pixel-identical. Retain direct, blended and source versions.
8. Check full scene against measured composite and masters: framing, rim, underside, apparent camera, identity, anatomy, equipment, scale, grounding/hover, clearance, materials, lighting and depth. Separate estimates from measurements; no exact camera inference from raster. Uniform normalization cannot fix relative scale. Artistic approval is separate from structural approval.
9. Add approved effects for production, omit during validation unless needed; apply final background and review full size and actual uncropped 256 × 256 preview. Mobile reviews concise, one main image and separate links.

## Alternatives
Use V2 controlled compositing (experimental), integrated foundation generation, or optional full-scene photographic generation when crop assembly is unsuitable. Mounted, mechanical, integrated and aerial assets require native staging, not forced ground contact. Full-scene generation uses measured composite for arrangement, approved assets for identity and Fighter for photographic quality. Stop after two unsuccessful full-scene attempts and review; further attempts require explicit approval.

## Supporting production toolkit

The approved [Original Island V3.1 toolkit](original-island-production-blueprint-v3.md) provides calibrated structural exports, placement guides, masks, coordinate metadata, photographic crop extraction, measured composite preparation, deterministic reinsertion and pixel-preservation verification.

Use it for the original-island configuration and adapt it cautiously for future encounters. Encounter portability and real edited-crop artistic integration remain under validation. The toolkit supports, but does not replace, Hybrid Assembly V3 approval gates or measured structural composite authority.
