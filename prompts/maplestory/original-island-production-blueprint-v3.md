## Authority hierarchy

1. Current canonical island and Hybrid Assembly V3 specifications govern production.
2. Approved artistic masters govern composition, materials and photographic quality within their respective scopes.
3. Encounter-specific approved structural configurations govern spatial planning.
4. Approved photographic foundations govern actual scenery and image pixels.
5. Measured structural composites govern final character arrangement.
6. Generated localized crops supply artistic content but do not override measured placement.
7. Historical experiments provide supporting evidence and limitations.

The neutral Blender scene is a structural planning and measurement tool, not an artistic appearance authority.

Artistic approval and structural approval remain independent.

## Production usage

Follow the approved Hybrid Assembly V3 workflow.

1. Establish and approve an encounter-specific composition blueprint.
2. Prepare and approve a photographic foundation.
3. Export calibrated placement guides.
4. Extract clean photographic crops and matching silhouette guides programmatically.
5. Record source identity, crop origin, dimensions, padding, intended bounds and anchors.
6. Perform independently approved localized artwork edits.
7. Normalize and register approved character assets.
8. Establish the measured structural composite.
9. Reinsert approved crops using deterministic image-processing tools.
10. Validate structure, protected pixels, seams, lighting, materials and photographic continuity.

Use Blender for geometry and placement guidance. Use image-processing scripts for exact pixel operations.

Never regenerate approved artwork merely to simplify registration or composition.

The V3.1 delivery reported:

- Preservation of 240 historical files.
- Passing results for all 52 inherited structural regression checks.
- 54 passing native pixel comparisons.
- 20 passing image-processing tests.

The subsequent review recorded independent checks of delivery hashes, baseline projection equality, reopened projection equality, nine restored visibility-preset renders, exact photographic crop extraction and unchanged-crop reinsertion.

Photographic round-trip tests and synthetic edited-crop tests supported preservation outside authorized regions for tested feather widths.

Native Blender execution and geometry validation were supported by delivered Codex records and were not independently rerun during the receiving-chat review.

These results validate the delivered configuration and tested operations, not universal cross-encounter performance.

## Configurable parameters

The following remain adjustable and encounter-specific:

- Camera and foundation geometry for future encounters.
- Character scale and native staging.
- Boss core and extended silhouette proportions.
- Crop boundaries and context padding.
- Protected-region masks.
- Overlap handling.
- Feather width.
- Environmental layout and action clearance.

The demonstrated 48 px crop padding and tested 0, 20, 40 and 60 px feather widths are experimental settings, not permanent defaults.

The final artwork canvas remains 1254 × 1254, with background #F3E6DD and no exterior cast shadow.

## Remaining limitations

- Original-island component registration is not yet generalized to arbitrary encounters.
- Real approved transparent-character compositing was not demonstrated in the delivered V3.1 dry run.
- Static proxy anchors do not establish anatomically verified boot contact.
- Physical proxy anatomy and detailed character depth remain provisional.
- Dynamic action and weapon-path clearance remain unvalidated.
- Historical foundation and boss silhouette overlap targets remain partially unmet.
- Stair construction and unified physical terrain remain simplified.
- Real edited-crop artistic integration and cross-encounter repeatability require further validation.

Do not conceal these limitations or reinterpret reference-specific fitting results as independent generalization evidence.

## Unchanged standards

V3.1 does not supersede the provisional V2B series-wide geometry and scale registry.

No universal hero height, head-to-body ratio, boss ratio, camera elevation, physical foundation depth, crop size or feather width is established by this approval.

All existing artistic masters, approved checkpoints and independent Hero and Boss Style Master development remain protected.

Island-specific artistic standards override cubby mascot rules.
