## Resolved authored prompt
Create one encounter-ready version of the supplied Ice/Lightning Mage miniature. Preserve her recognizable face, lavender eyes, cheerful confident expression, blue sculpted plush-like bangs and flowing curls, black bow, blue-white embroidered costume, footwear, crystal staff and ornate open spellbook. Preserve existing compact anatomy rather than redesigning it. Rotate head, gaze and torso naturally toward the right, with a three-quarter combat orientation inspired by Fighter. Spread arms in an active casting gesture: forward hand grips staff aimed diagonally toward the intended opponent on the right; opposite arm extends outward, open relaxed palm. The open book levitates independently above and slightly behind that free hand on the opposite side, with visible air gap, no physical support or contact, clear of hair and face. Grounded staggered stance, slight forward weight shift. Preserve soft photographic lighting, controlled saturation, fine directional hair fibers, smooth matte facial contours and distinct textile, leather, crystal and metal finishes. Low nearly side-on camera, complete silhouette and equipment visible. No boss, terrain, spell particles, beams, circles, ground plane or exterior shadow. One isolated image, final 1254 × 1254 canvas, #F3E6DD background.
