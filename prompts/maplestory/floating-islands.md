## Floating presentation — no exterior shadow
The island floats freely in an uninterrupted background. No ground plane, exterior cast shadow, grounding shadow, drop shadow or reflection beneath the island. Preserve natural internal contact shadows between characters, scenery and terrain, and natural shading on the island underside.

## Reference hierarchy
Approved composition master controls framing, apparent camera, silhouette, character/environment proportions, combat space and environmental depth; texture master controls materials only, not scale, pose, class identity or composition. Preserve both without regeneration. Extract and inspect the relevant guides, Blender geometry and measurements from `V2B_Camera_Geometry_Guide_Package.zip` in Project Sources (provisional). If unavailable, state the limitation and request the guide; do not substitute legacy geometry. Historical conversations, attachments, failed experiments and superseded specs are for learning only, not active visual authorities unless explicitly requested. Prefer dedicated versioned reference sheets with complete examples and close-ups; create additional masters only when needed.

Maintain shared world scale. Standing hero calibration of 225 px is provisional. Distinguish standing anatomy, posed height, equipment silhouette, mounted assembly and boss extensions. Do not enlarge figures to fill space. Boss scaling exceptions require explicit approval. Reserve room for melee, ranged, summons, machinery and aerial action.

## Map interpretation
Interpret recognizable MapleStory maps as standalone themed floating islands rather than literal miniature reproductions of gameplay maps. Preserve their distinctive materials, colors, vegetation, architecture and atmosphere. Omit gameplay platforms, ropes, enclosing ceilings and other navigation structures unless they meaningfully contribute to the island's physical design and visual identity.

## Composition-aware foundation planning
Before foundation generation, approve a lightweight composition-aware spatial blueprint on provisional V2B geometry: approximate posed hero and boss silhouettes, footing, combat lane and visible scenery zones. For large, mounted, mechanical or aerial assemblies, include full projected silhouettes, weapon, wing and tail clearance as relevant. Plan distinctive scenery where it remains readable around characters. Placeholders guide foundation generation but must not appear in the foundation; integrated bosses remain physically integrated. Environment-only islands need no character placeholders. This planning blueprint does not replace the later measured structural composite. Apply from Validation 04 onward; do not restart Validation 03.

## Large-boss staging
Prefer a slightly recessed footing position for large bosses when it improves visual mass distribution, foreground flooring, combat clearance and environmental depth, as observed in Paladin × Zakum and Wild Hunter × Horntail. This is an optional staging preference, not a universal depth rule. Keep physically plausible footing and readable silhouettes; validate against the encounter blueprint and measured composite. Do not change approved anatomy or scale to achieve this effect.

## Mandatory workflow
Follow [Hybrid Assembly V3](hybrid-assembly-workflow-v3.md). Obtain explicit approval at each generation/edit checkpoint; preserve approved assets, report drift and measured versus estimated values. Keep mobile reviews concise, one main image and separate links. Validation-only encounters may omit effects.

Compare width, center rim, rear playable edge, underside, apparent camera and silhouette clearance against approved foundation and relevant guide at equal canvas size. Report measured vs estimated values. Check character identity, anatomy, equipment, grounding, materials, lighting, depth and action clearance before advancing. Existing approved artwork and encounter-specific locked cameras remain unchanged unless separately approved.
