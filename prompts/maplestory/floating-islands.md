# MapleStory floating islands — V2.1

> **Island V2.1 (approved 2026-11-23):** Standalone islands use premium handcrafted miniature collectibles, not cubby plush. `MASTER_Island_Composition_V1.jpeg` controls composition; `MASTER_Island_Character_Texture_V1` controls character materials only (approved PNG or JPEG copy). Hair is sculpted with subtle grooves; faces smooth matte anime with class-specific eyes; fabrics fine and restrained; metal satin-matte; leather subtle grain; fur short and controlled; machinery distinct painted and metallic surfaces. No Jolly faces, button eyes, yarn hair, excessive fuzz or glossy plastic. Wild Hunter and Mechanic cubby masters are historical only for islands unless explicitly requested. Cubby rules remain unchanged. See [floating islands](floating-islands.md).

## Reference hierarchy
Approved composition master controls framing, apparent camera, silhouette, character/environment proportions, combat space and environmental depth; texture master controls materials only, not scale, pose, class identity or composition. Preserve both without regeneration. Extract and inspect the relevant guides, Blender geometry and measurements from `V2B_Camera_Geometry_Guide_Package.zip` in Project Sources (provisional). If unavailable, state the limitation and request the guide; do not substitute legacy geometry. Historical conversations, attachments, failed experiments and superseded specs are for learning only, not active visual authorities unless explicitly requested. Prefer dedicated versioned reference sheets with complete examples and close-ups; create additional masters only when needed.

## Mandatory workflow
1. Read current canonical specs and inspect masters and relevant extracted guides. Gather hero, boss, map, weapons, skills and summons; use class references for identity and equipment.
2. Present detailed composition, lighting, scale and action plan; wait for explicit approval before any generation or edit.
3. Validate geometry, scale, grounding and combat clearance with Blender when needed; Blender is structural, not final artistic rendering.
4. Establish and approve empty island foundation. Record framing and floor depth.
5. Develop and approve cohesive scenery. Preserve map identity, physically connected foreground, midground and distant scenery, believable proportions, overlap and subtle atmospheric depth. Keep combat lanes open; prefer cohesive generation when separate layers compromise continuity.
6. Add and approve characters using the island texture master. Check anatomy, grip, gaze, equipment, scale, contact or hover shadows and lighting.
7. Add and approve effects, summons and attacks. Preserve readability and clean base; keep effects localized.
8. Apply final #F3E6DD background; review full-size and actual uncropped 256 × 256 gallery preview. No text, logos, labels or UI.

Generate one image per approved step. Maintain editable layers and checkpoints where technically possible; preserve approved components. Prefer isolated edits or compositing when feasible, but do not sacrifice environmental continuity. Never claim pixel preservation for regenerated imagery; identify and correct drift. Use consistent soft lighting, natural contact shadows and material-specific finishes. Keep mobile review to one main image, concise measurements and separate links; avoid embedded side-by-side thumbnails.

## Checkpoints
Compare width, center rim, rear playable edge, underside, apparent camera and silhouette clearance against approved foundation and relevant guide at equal canvas size. Report measured vs estimated values. Check character identity, anatomy, equipment, grounding, materials, lighting, depth and action clearance before advancing. Existing approved artwork and encounter-specific locked cameras remain unchanged unless separately approved.
