**Jolly identity:** compact earless cream plush mascot, broad smooth finely velvety face with subtle short microfibers distinct from fluffy surrounding cream fur, small dark embroidered/bead eyes with tiny highlights, subtle blush, simple expressive mouth. Dimensional sculpted textile hair; costumes and weapons accurate to references. No human/anime makeover.

**V4 Wild Hunter material lock:** match approved Wild Hunter vs Giant Blackheart's handmade tactile feel across face, hair, clothing, boss, props, terrain, and environment. Fine plush fibers on soft surfaces; textile woven details and stitching; appropriately matte miniature painted metal on swords, armor and mechanical parts (never furred-up robots). Diffuse highlights, preserve glowing magic/translucent effects; avoid shiny plastic CGI.

**Cubby:** rigid sharp beveled square cubby, fully framed with four outer margins, ceiling and side walls visible. Small thin circular warm-white LED disc exactly centered and flush in ceiling, narrow cream ring, soft warm falloff; no bulky lamp. Match **`MASTER_Cubby_Bulbasaur.png`** as primary for cubby wall/frame colors and shadow; **`MASTER_Cubby_Secondary_Link.png`** is secondary for cubby consistency. Do not use Buccaneer to infer cubby colors; it is a face/expression reference only. Use original Black Clover only as legacy geometry master if explicitly specified.
