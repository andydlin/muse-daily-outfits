## Workflow

**Brainstorm the pairing and staging first and obtain approval before rendering.** Where creating sequential pairs, vary/alternate facing direction, action silhouette and gaze rather than repeating poses. Class references determine costume/hair/weapon details; boss references determine monster silhouette. Neither controls the image's plush rendering style.

**Weapon accuracy — mandatory during planning; skill selection optional:** Identify the class's actual signature weapon, its physical construction (hilt, handle, blade, mechanism or other defining form), grip, movement and relationship to the intended action before proposing the scene. Do not require the user to select a hero skill or boss attack for cubby scenes; those separate reference-selection steps belong to the floating-island workflow. Clearly distinguish physical weapon components from surrounding magical/energy effects. Do not replace a characteristic weapon with generic glowing arcs, ordinary swords or decorative effects. If source references are ambiguous, verify the class-specific weapon interpretation before rendering. In the scene proposal, explicitly describe the weapon silhouette and how the action reads.

**First-pass staging checklist:** Describe the hero's visual size and foreground position, boss size and depth/placement, class-specific weapon silhouette, hairstyle translation into plush, facing/gaze, and readable effects where appropriate. Do not ask the user to pick a specific hero skill or boss attack for cubby scenes. Obtain approval before generating.

## Generate

Create **one square standalone photorealistic 3D plush miniature diorama** of **Gizmo as a MapleStory-inspired [CLASS]** confronting **[BOSS]** in **[APPROVED POSE / ACTION]**, with optional class-appropriate magical effects. A named signature skill is included only if specifically requested or approved.

Gizmo and the primary weapon/action silhouette generally occupy approximately **75–85% of the usable cubby interior**, as appropriate to the pose. **Hero dominance is critical on the first pass:** favor a substantial, unmistakably foreground hero, with the face, costume, weapon and action silhouette immediately readable. **Scale flexibility:** allow modest variation for class silhouette, weapon size and dynamic posing; do not force identical hero sizes across scenes. **Boss hierarchy:** place the boss visibly secondary in size and typically deeper in the mid/background, preserving enough distinctive silhouette, face, weapon and effects for clear recognition. Maintain deliberate separation and negative space between hero and boss. Do not enlarge the hero by cropping the cubby or its margins; preserve the approved framing. Stage genuine layered depth with class-specific pose, intact hand/weapon grips, room for effects and environment. Do not achieve scale by cropping the cubby. Avoid reusing prior combat poses.

**MapleStory face priority — CRITICAL, apply on the FIRST PASS:** Before interpreting class-specific styling, establish the correct face from `MASTER_Maple_Face_Ice_Lightning_Mage.png` (primary) and `MASTER_Maple_Face_Secondy_Buccaneer.png` (secondary). Reinforce the eye shape using `MASTER_Texture_Wild_Hunter.jpg` and `MASTER_Texture_Mechanic.png` as **supplemental eye-only references**. Eyes should be **small-to-medium, softly rounded embroidered/button-like eyes with minimal internal iris detail and tiny restrained white catchlights**. **Default eye color is dark brown/black; an approved class-specific eye color (such as Demon Slayer's red eyes) may be retained when essential to that character's recognizable design.** The color exception affects pigment only, never the soft rounded eye geometry, restrained catchlights, or fine plush construction. Retain familiar Jolly eye spacing, plush facial proportions, rosy cheeks, and a simple expressive mouth; use eyebrows, gaze direction and mouth to convey emotion. **No generic colored anime irises, oversized or complex glossy irises, pointy human/anime eye outlines, heavy lashes, or shiny plastic faces.** Class-specific colored eyes are permitted **only when supported by the supplied reference or already approved design**, and must remain simplified, rounded, embroidered-looking, and subtly highlighted. This is a facial style constraint, not a restriction on the colors of the boss, attacks or outfit. **Before finalizing, check that the hero face matches the primary/secondary MapleStory masters more closely than generic anime/chibi art; repair mismatched eyes without changing approved costume, scale, scene, pose, effects or cubby.**

**Jolly identity:** compact earless cream plush mascot, broad smooth finely velvety face with subtle short microfibers distinct from fluffy surrounding cream fur, small dark embroidered/bead eyes with tiny highlights, subtle blush, simple expressive mouth. Dimensional sculpted textile hair; costumes and weapons accurate to references. No human/anime makeover.

**Plush hairstyle interpretation:** Retain each class's recognizable hairstyle and silhouette, but construct it as coherent, substantial, rounded sculpted plush-fabric tufts with visible textile grain and softness. Avoid defaulting to excessive thin, sharp, layered anime spikes or hard-plastic hair. Preserve intentional spikes where the source design specifically calls for them. A rounded, slightly tousled bowl cut is **Xenon-specific guidance, not a universal hairstyle requirement**.

**V4 Wild Hunter material lock:** match approved Wild Hunter vs Giant Blackheart's handmade tactile feel across face, hair, clothing, boss, props, terrain, and environment. Fine plush fibers on soft surfaces; textile woven details and stitching; appropriately matte miniature painted metal on swords, armor and mechanical parts (never furred-up robots). Diffuse highlights, preserve glowing magic/translucent effects; avoid shiny plastic CGI.

**Cubby:** rigid sharp beveled square cubby, fully framed with four outer margins, ceiling and side walls visible. Small thin circular warm-white LED disc exactly centered and flush in ceiling, narrow cream ring, soft warm falloff; no bulky lamp. Match **`MASTER_Cubby_Bulbasaur.png`** as primary for cubby wall/frame colors and shadow; **`MASTER_Cubby_Secondary_Link.png`** is secondary for cubby consistency. Do not use Buccaneer to infer cubby colors; it is a face/expression reference only. Use original Black Clover only as legacy geometry master if explicitly specified. Maintain scene depth and natural lighting.

Return **ONE image**, no numbered plaque, title, label, text, UI, branding or side-by-side.
