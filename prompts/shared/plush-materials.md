# Shared spec — Handmade miniature material language

- Photorealistic **3D handmade plush miniature diorama**; tactile, premium, camera-photographed miniature rather than 2D illustration, shiny PVC collectible or generic CGI.
- Fine textile fibers/nap on soft cream face (while preserving Jolly's smooth inset silhouette), fuller fibers in framing cream fur; dimensional hair with directional woven/yarn texture.
- Clothing, capes, costumes and soft props show felt/weave texture, seams and delicate stitches. Scenery, soil, stone, foliage and monsters use cohesive handcrafted miniature textures appropriate to each object.
- Armor, swords, robots and mechanical equipment stay **recognizably hard-surface metal** with matte painted miniature finish, coherent edges, joints, bolts and subtle micrograin. **Do not make machinery furry.**
- Prefer soft diffuse material highlights instead of glossy, hard-plastic speculars. Magical glow, transparent visors, fire, electricity, glass and similar effects remain luminous or translucent.
- Keep surfaces consistent across hero, boss and terrain rather than rendering the hero as cloth against a smooth toy boss.
- Avoid overly coarse fabric/bouclé, oversharpened noise, plastic skin, flat pasted-on hair and two-dimensional scenery.

## MapleStory V4 texture anchor

When generating a new MapleStory confrontation, **attach the approved Wild Hunter vs Giant Blackheart image** and treat it as primary **material/texture** reference. It is not permission to copy the Wild Hunter outfit, pose, boss or scene. Preserve character-specific designs from their own references.
