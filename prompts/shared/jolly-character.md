# Shared spec — Muse Jolly identity

**Applies:** All daily outfit and MapleStory hero scenes, including face-only corrections.

## Locked identity

- Compact, rounded, soft plush body; short rounded plush limbs and **no anatomical ears**.
- Broad, rounded **smooth inset cream face**, subtly velvety with **fine short textile nap** — not bare plastic/vinyl. Distinct ring of **fluffier cream head fur** frames the face.
- Small, fairly spaced black bead-like / softly embroidered dark eyes with restrained tiny highlights; subtle rosy cheeks; simple small curved or expressive mouth. Keep underlying recognizable Jolly proportions across themes.
- No elongated muzzle, oversized anime eyes, lash-heavy eyes, or human face. No shaggy/bouclé/woolly face; the **surrounding fur**, not the smooth facial inset, provides the visible fluff.
- Hair and hairstyles added by costume are **separate dimensional sculpted plush-fabric tufts**, with textile grain, thickness and contact shadows, never flat painted/illustrated hair or sharp plastic spikes.
- Creature-themed costume hoods **may have attached costume ears** and should carry appropriate embroidered eyes/facial markings **on the hood** while Jolly's own face remains visible.
- Use approved Jett — Wind Dash, Zelda — Triforce, Demon Slayer, and corrected Fighter references for Jolly identity when available. A face-only repair must not alter pose, clothing, props, environment, scene, or framing.

## Context-sensitive finish

Daily outfit faces prioritize the smooth velvety inset and fluffy framing ring. MapleStory V4 adds Wild Hunter's **fine visible microfibers**, still not shaggy or plastic-looking. These are complementary, not competing, requirements.

## Daily Outfit Jett two-layer face construction and expression independence — approved Yuji Black Flash refinement (2026-11-11)

For **Daily Outfits**, the face must visibly read as **two distinct plush surfaces**, not one uniformly furry face:

1. **Inner facial inset:** broad, softly rounded/oval cream area with an exceptionally smooth, fine, short velvety nap; very little individually discernible fiber. It has its own subtle rounded contour, not a painted outline or plastic mask. Eyes, mouth and cheek blush belong on this inset.
2. **Outer cream head-fur ring:** continuous, visibly fluffier cream border with modestly longer fibers, clearly separated from the inset by natural contour, fiber length and material contrast. The ring sits beneath costume hair or hood and must remain legible even with dramatic expressions.
3. **Separate costume hair:** sculpted textile tufts must not merge into the face inset or the cream fur ring.

**Expression independence:** Preserve the scene-approved emotional expression, including serious/angry angled brows, intense small Jolly eyes, and a fierce open-mouth shout when appropriate. Never replace a serious expression with a cheerful smile just to restore Jolly identity. Facial geometry and material hierarchy remain Jett-like regardless of expression. Avoid oversized anime eyes or humanized face anatomy.

**Reference priority remains unchanged:** `MASTER_Jolly_Face_Jett.png` primary; `MASTER_Jolly_Face_Secondy_Goku.png` secondary. The approved Yuji Black Flash Option 1 is an example of **serious expression with two-layer Jolly face construction**, **not** a replacement master. MapleStory-specific face/eye masters apply only to MapleStory scenes; Wild Hunter controls overall handcrafted material language but must not overwrite the Daily Outfit face structure.

For a **face-only correction**, modify only inner inset smoothness/shape, outer fur-ring separation and any explicitly requested expression details. Preserve the approved expression unless a change is requested; keep hair, pose, costume, energy effects, environment, cubby, lighting, scale and composition unchanged.

## Locked short-pile Jolly material hierarchy — Tyranitar refinement (2026-11-10)

Jolly is a **premium short-pile plush collectible**, **never a long-haired or shaggy stuffed animal**. Maintain this hierarchy for **both Daily Outfits and MapleStory**:

| Area | Approved surface |
| --- | --- |
| Cream face inset (cheeks and forehead) | **Smoothest surface:** very fine, even, velvet-like short plush nap; no coarse or long directional strands, but not plastic/vinyl |
| Cream fur ring framing the face/hood opening | **Slightly fluffier** than the face, with soft, modestly longer fibers; distinct from the smooth facial inset |
| Exposed cream body (belly, arms, hands, legs, feet) | **Short, dense, uniform plush pile**, subtle tactile fiber detail, rounded silhouette; never shaggy or stringy |
| Costumes / hoodies | Appropriate woven or felt textile grain, seams and visible stitching |
| Armor, horns and spikes | Cohesive structured matte handmade miniature materials, not body fur |

Use **`MASTER_Cubby_Bulbasaur.png`** as a supplemental **Jolly fine-plush surface** reference in addition to its primary cubby role. Compare the face and exposed cream body to Bulbasaur for pile length, density and visual softness. **Jett remains the primary Jolly face/identity master**, Goku secondary; Bulbasaur does not override face proportions, eyes, expressions, costume or pose. Wild Hunter remains the overall handmade diorama texture master.

When refining plush pile length on an approved image, adjust **only the specified face/body/fur surfaces**; keep the original eyes, expression, proportions, pose, costume, props, environment, effects, cubby colors, LED and framing unchanged. Do not flatten all surface materials to the same texture.
