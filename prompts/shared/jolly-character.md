# Shared spec — Muse Jolly identity

**Applies:** All daily outfit and MapleStory hero scenes, including face-only corrections.

## Locked identity

- Compact, rounded, soft plush body; short rounded plush limbs and **no anatomical ears**.
- Broad, rounded **smooth inset cream face**, subtly velvety with **fine short textile nap** — not bare plastic/vinyl. Distinct ring of **fluffier cream head fur** frames the face.
- Small, fairly spaced black bead-like / softly embroidered dark eyes with restrained tiny highlights; subtle rosy cheeks; simple small curved or expressive mouth. Keep underlying recognizable Jolly proportions across themes.
- No elongated muzzle, oversized anime eyes, lash-heavy eyes, or human face. No shaggy/bouclé/woolly face; the **surrounding fur**, not the smooth facial inset, provides the visible fluff.
- Hair and hairstyles added by costume are **separate dimensional sculpted plush-fabric tufts**, with textile grain, thickness and contact shadows, never flat painted/illustrated hair or sharp plastic spikes.
- Creature-themed costume hoods **may have attached costume ears** and should carry appropriate embroidered eyes/facial markings **on the hood** while Jolly's own face remains visible.
- Use approved Jett — Wind Dash, Zelda — Triforce, Demon Slayer, and corrected Fighter references for Jolly identity when available. A face-only repair must not alter pose, clothing, props, environment, scene, or framing.

## Context-sensitive finish

Daily outfit faces prioritize the smooth velvety inset and fluffy framing ring. MapleStory V4 adds Wild Hunter's **fine visible microfibers**, still not shaggy or plastic-looking. These are complementary, not competing, requirements.

## Locked short-pile Jolly material hierarchy — Tyranitar refinement (2026-11-10)

Jolly is a **premium short-pile plush collectible**, **never a long-haired or shaggy stuffed animal**. Maintain this hierarchy for **both Daily Outfits and MapleStory**:

| Area | Approved surface |
| --- | --- |
| Cream face inset (cheeks and forehead) | **Smoothest surface:** very fine, even, velvet-like short plush nap; no coarse or long directional strands, but not plastic/vinyl |
| Cream fur ring framing the face/hood opening | **Slightly fluffier** than the face, with soft, modestly longer fibers; distinct from the smooth facial inset |
| Exposed cream body (belly, arms, hands, legs, feet) | **Short, dense, uniform plush pile**, subtle tactile fiber detail, rounded silhouette; never shaggy or stringy |
| Costumes / hoodies | Appropriate woven or felt textile grain, seams and visible stitching |
| Armor, horns and spikes | Cohesive structured matte handmade miniature materials, not body fur |
